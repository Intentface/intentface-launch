# Animation patterns

Moves that recur in product launch videos, each built and checked once in this engine, so a shot that needs one starts from a working version instead of from nothing. The code is in the packages' repo, `videos/example-<family>/video.tsx` (<https://github.com/Intentface/intentface-launch/tree/main/videos>): one shot per pattern, each a small component whose copy and cue frames are props, with a JSDoc that gives its numbers and why. They are drawn on a neutral made-up app, light, or dark inside `<ExampleTheme value={DARK}>` (`videos/example-look.tsx`); in a product video the same moves run on the product's own UI (a `Snapshot`'s `animate`, `api.type`, `api.textRects`), in its look.

A pattern is a starting point for a beat the script already has, not a reason to add a beat. Pick by what the story needs to show, then take the numbers.

To see one: `pnpm launch sheet example-<family> --shot <id> --every 3` in the packages' repo.

## AI products (`example-ai`)

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Cut in on the caret (`cut-in`) | The words being typed are the point (a prompt, a query) and too small to read wide | Typing at 13 characters a second (`typingCadence`). On the keystroke nearest a beat that falls inside a word, the view jumps in one frame to 2× closer (the wide view already frames the heading, field and chips at 1.42×, so the prompt goes from 57 to 114 px) with the caret on the same point of the screen, and the typing carries straight on (a key on the cut frame, the next a few frames later), so it reads as leaning in, not a new scene. Much under 2× the cut looks like a mistake; over 3× it is a new shot. Close in, the view follows the caret on `springTo` (`{ duration: 40 }`, one target per keystroke) so it glides instead of stepping per letter. Then a cut back out wide, where the cursor rests 14 frames on send and clicks on the beat. With a still close view, Camera keys with `cut: true` make both cuts; this one follows the caret, so the shot computes its view per frame. |
| Streaming answer (`stream`) | Any generated text: an answer, a summary, a draft | Open close on the send button as it is pressed and cut hard to the answer on the click. Words 3 to 6 frames apart (seeded), 6 more at a new line, each fading in over 21 frames from a 1 px blur. Warmth runs on words arrived, not frames: a word stays in the accent while the next 4 arrive, then cools to ink over 4 more, so five or six words are warm whatever the speed. The camera pulls back from 2.2× from half a beat after the cut to the whole exchange, on `ease.swap`: `ease.inOut`, or a pass and return, leaves the text moving under a pixel a frame for half a second, and it steps. Copy and Retry rise in once the last word has landed. |
| Select a passage (`select`) | Acting on part of a text: rewrite, explain, comment | Word boxes measured once the font has loaded (in a product: `api.textRects`). The selection band is `edges()`: its leading edge snaps to a word's end once the pointer passes that word's middle (about 10 frames a word), never between words. The action chip arrives 5 frames after the release, from 0.97 over 15 frames `ease.out` (a surface: no overshoot), beside the selection when it ends its line, else above. One camera push, to about 1.5×, before the click. |
| Agent steps (`agent-steps`) | An agent working through a task | A log writes itself a step at a time, 16 to 58 frames a step, uneven like real work. Each step's spinner swaps to a check drawn over 18 frames as its text dims over 12; the next starts as the last finishes. From the sixth line the list scrolls on a spring (`{ duration: 45 }`), one target per step, so it carries its speed from step to step; older lines fade under the title bar. The result line holds for its reading time. |

## UI changing state (`example-states`)

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Status pill that cycles (`status`) | A state the product reports changes, in a sentence: "Your build is queued → running → live" | The pill's width on `springTo` (`{ duration: 27, bounce: 0.3 }`, rounded to even pixels), colour on `springColor` (18 frames, no bounce). The label rolls: old and new on one clock, one pill height apart, 18 frames `ease.out`, so they never overlap. When the pill grows the label trails the width by 4 frames; when it shrinks the label goes first and the width follows 8 frames later, so the label's sides are never clipped. The prefix re-centres from the same width every frame, so it glides, never jumps. |
| State spreading through a list (`spread`) | One action changes many things: access granted to a team, a fix applied to every row | The cursor rests 14 frames, clicks on the beat; the button dips 1 → 0.97 → 1 and becomes "Access granted" (shape on springs, label 3 frames behind). From click + 12, a wave runs down the rows 7 frames apart: avatar fills over 12 frames, then a badge pops (`overshootEase(0.25)`), the row swells 2% about its baseline. A soft band travelling with the front makes the rows one ripple, not a list of events. |
| A task finishing (`progress-toast`) | Something the product does takes a moment and then is done: an export, a deploy, a sync | The toast rises in, counts unevenly to 100% (tabular figures, bar following), holds 8 frames, then morphs as one shape: content out in 6 frames, shape shrinks from +4, result in at +8, check draws from +13 (it shrinks, so content goes first). Then the window recedes and a short headline rises from behind it into its own space. |
| A conversation filling in (`thread`) | Chat, comments, a team at work | Before each message, three dots pulse in a wave (a 36-frame loop, each dot a sixth of a cycle behind) for about a beat; the message rises 8 px out of a 1 px blur. The list's height is one `springTo` (`{ duration: 24 }`), retargeted when someone starts typing and when the message lands, so pushes overlap without stopping. Uneven gaps, like a real conversation. |

## Kinetic type (`example-type`)

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| A line that stays centred as it builds (`build-centred`) | A claim told word by word on the beats | Each word lands on a beat (`Reveal` with `times`, 24 frames, starting 6 frames early). Widths from `useMeasureText`; the line's left edge on `springTo` (`{ duration: 18, bounce: 0 }`), retargeted per word so the re-centres overlap at speed; `landed()` puts the held line on whole pixels (0.035 px judder, against 0.28 px rounding every frame). |
| A sentence that finishes itself several ways (`slot`) | One product, many uses: "Make a video for your launch / release / team" | A fixed lead-in, the last word on a reel. Steps on beats on `springTo` (`{ duration: 20 }`), then a spin: a 12-frame ramp up and `brake()` over 48 frames onto the last word, on a beat, top speed solved so ramp and brake meet. `MotionBlur` only above 4 px a frame (32 samples: 16 showed copies). The slot's width on `{ duration: 12, bounce: 0 }` so the lead-in follows; a feathered window so neighbours do not show. |
| Wordmark, then a cut to its last letters (`wordmark-cut`, `wordmark-close`) | An end card that should feel final | The wordmark flies in from 3.2× (log space, about its baseline) with a 1500 px slide over 72 frames `ease.expo`, blur on the first 30; colour cools from the accent to ink over 48 frames. It holds about 80 frames, then a hard cut on the beat to the same layout 1.7× nearer, the full stop right of centre, held still. |
| Statement cards that cut hard (`punch`) | A short manifesto, three beats of claim | Each line flicks 1.2 em in from the left over 18 frames `ease.expo`, no bounce (0.6 em read as a pop); the second line 5 frames behind. Each card lasts its reading time rounded up to whole beats, so every cut lands on one. Vary size, place and ground between cards so the cuts read as rhythm. |

## Camera and composition (`example-camera`)

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| A field wider than the frame (`wide-field`) | Typing a long query, prompt or command close up | The field at its real size, filmed at about 2.4× so the frame shows two thirds of it. The pan sets off while the caret is still 2 characters short of the safe margin (the pan eases in, so starting at the edge lets the caret spill out), over 48 frames on a gentle in-out (`bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1)`: `ease.inOut` let the caret gain 59 px on the frame), under motion blur. Typing never pauses for it. |
| Cards flicked along a rail (`flick`) | Browsing templates, a gallery, a catalogue | Speed rises over 5 frames, then `brake()` over 84 frames; the peak is solved from the distance, so the rail stops exactly one or two cards on, with no overshoot. Blur only above a quarter of the peak speed. Each card in its own perspective (1400 px, tilted 22°): one shared vanishing point made the side cards' type look italic. |
| Proof by multiplication (`count-grid`) | A number that matters: teams, projects, rows processed | The grid rushes in at 60 px a frame under blur and brakes onto three cards over 48 frames; the label plate sits just above them, its content trailing 3 frames. Then cells fill outward from the centre, 5 frames per cell of distance, while the camera pulls back 1.7 → 1.2 over 60 frames (shorter than the count, so the final number is held still) and `Count` runs 3 → 2,480 on `ease.inOut` to keep pace with the fill. |
| A wall of integrations (`wall`) | "Works with everything you use" | The headline on its own plate holds still in the middle. Tiles on a sunflower spiral (radius 140·√n, golden angle) flow outward from behind it, the index growing 0.1 a frame, so the field stays evenly filled; the camera pulls back 1.3 → 0.72 at an even rate in log space, so new tiles keep arriving from every edge. |

## Shaders around and on UI (`example-shaders`)

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Middle card, frosted (`hero-frosted`) | A hero section or feature card set in light: a website's hero, a product's landing | `MiddleCard` (kit) over a `Shaders` layer (Satin in orange): the card 1500 × 820, radius 44, frosted glass (blur 36 px, a dark tint at 22%, a hairline edge). It opens on frame 1 over 30 frames from 96%, the blur coming in with it; the headline reveals 14 frames in. The site's nav and button are UI (`data-launch-check="off"`). |
| Middle card, a window (`hero-window`) | A product's own gradient hero, shown as its site would | `blur="around"`: the card shows the shader (a MeshGradient) sharp; round it the same shader blurs to 64 px and washes pale (a pink tint at 45%) as the card opens, so the card is the subject. |
| Fluid type (`fluid-type`) | A word that reacts to a cursor: "it responds", a playful headline | The word is a `textPicture` in a `Shaders` layer under `CursorRipples` (intensity 9, decay 4, chromatic split 1.2); the layer's `pointer` is the kit cursor's own path (`cursorAt(keys, f)`), and the `Cursor` is drawn `frosted` on top. The ripples follow the cursor and settle in about a second. |
| Liquid metal (`liquid-metal`) | Premium UI: a button, a toggle, a badge | `LiquidMetal` (Paper's shader) in a ring round the button (the pill less its face, drawn on a canvas, with 16 px of room inside its own canvas), the face and label in the DOM. `matte` 0 splits colour along the bends; 1 is satin. Show it close: 1,200 px wide or more, with a slow push. |

## Broadcast and analogue (`example-broadcast`)

Each picture is a pure function of the frame drawn with Canvas 2D (paths, fills, text; no shaders), shown on core's `Tube`. The pictures stand alone too (`bypass` on the Tube), and a pure `draw(ctx, frame)` runs the same in a browser player as in the renderer. Colours are broadcast colours, not the example look's; type is the look's faces. Drawing rules: svg-drawing skill.

| Pattern (shot) | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Power on (`power-on`) | A cold open: the set warms up on the first picture | `power={{ on: 6 }}`: dark glass, then the raster opens from a line to full height in 0.7 s (width in 0.16 s first), snow fading over 1.1 s; `degauss` a quarter second after, colours swimming for 1.8 s. The test card under it (bars, castellations, step wedge, gratings at 12, 6, 4 and 2 picture pixels, pluge, circle and a name plate) shows what the tube does: gratings soften, highlights swell between lines. |
| Station ident (`ident`) | A product's name over a "we are live" moment, a section card | A wireframe globe (latitudes every 15°, nine meridians, a tilted ring, a satellite) turning 0.32 rad a second, each segment drawn in front or behind by its depth (back at 22% opacity), beside a stacked wordmark sized to its space; a timecode and an "On air" tally blinking at 3.2 rad a second. Starts on `tune={[0]}`: 0.9 s of snow and a roll. |
| Teletext page (`teletext`) | A changelog, a schedule or a feature list told as a page of text | 40 × 25 cells in the seven teletext colours; each row a cell high (34 px at 1080, so every line reads), the title double height on blue, a rainbow bar cycling 6 cells a second, NEW flashing at 0.75 Hz, a coloured link row. Header, clock and links are not declared to `check` (furniture); the items are. |
| Vector scope (`scope`) | A signal, audio, a voice, a pulse of data | A 3 : 2 Lissajous figure on `phosphor="long"` (0.3 to 0.45 s): each frame draws only the stretch the beam covered since the last, and the phosphor makes the fading tail. The tube draws the 55 frames before for it, so the shot costs about 4× a `standard` one. |
| Station clock (`clock`) | A countdown to a launch: "Live at nine" | The clock is the frame (`start` seconds after midnight at frame 0), never the machine's. Sixty dots, the seconds filling amber, the second hand stepping once a second as a station clock's does; at `live` the line under the time turns from "Next" to "Now". |
| 80s tape intro (`chrome`) | A logo reveal that should feel like a VHS tape's opening | `tape={1}`, `phosphor="slow"`, snow 0.85 clearing by 0.75 s. PLAY in the corner; a laser grid running to a glowing horizon; the mark flies from the vanishing point over 1.9 s `easeOut3`, spinning on its axis (width as a cosine through 2.5 turns) with a 4-copy trail, flashes white, then moves aside over 0.9 s as the chrome letters rise in one by one (0.55 s each on a back ease, 0.06 s apart, seven extrusion copies under each). A light sweep every 4.5 s (the word drawn again in a moving band, `lighter`); the tagline types at 26 characters a second with a block caret. |
| Starfield, snow, off (`surf`) | An ending, or a cut between chapters | 520 stars on `phosphor="trails"`, each a seeded place coming forward over a cycle and a new place each time round (no state between frames); a channel change into snow (`tune`, `snow={0.88}`); `power={{ off }}`: the raster collapses to a line in 0.22 s, the line to a bright spot by 0.34 s, the spot fading by 0.9 s. |

- **Snow is what costs bits.** A steady picture on the tube encodes at 0.3 to 2.6 Mbit/s (the mask included); snow is new noise in every pixel every frame: 40 to 120 Mbit/s at the render's quality. Recompressed at CRF 30 it still takes 51 Mbit/s and smears into blotches, and a platform's bitrate cap does worse. Keep channel changes to their 0.9 s and no-signal stretches short.
- **The mask reads at 2 px.** A 6 px triad (`maskScale` 2, the default) still reads as a mask after a recompress at CRF 30 and a shrink to 640 px; at 1 px it blurs into stripes of two colours. Too fine for the render's scale (a contact sheet), the mask and scanlines show as their average.
- **Judge colour on stills, not sheets.** On a contact sheet (1/6 scale), Chrome's screenshot of the second and later tubes on a page comes out green; the canvas itself, stills at 0.25 or above and the render are right.

## What they taught

- **Measure before trusting the eye.** `pnpm launch measure` gave the numbers above: type scaled about its centre judders, rounding a spring every frame steps its tail, a slow pull-back makes held type creep. Crop to one element (`--crop`) before reading judder.
- **Content order follows the size change.** When a container grows, it moves first and the content follows; when it shrinks, the content goes first. Either way nothing is clipped.
- **Close enough to read, always.** Even where nothing needs reading, the UI's own text stays at 20 px or more on screen; a whole app framed wide is an establishing second at most. `check` warns (`ui`) otherwise.
- **A count tracks what it counts.** On `ease.out` a counter showed 919 while three cards were on screen; pace it with the picture.
