Update with `pnpm update @intentface-launch/core` (and the add-ons you use),
then `pnpm launch update` to refresh the skills and guides in `launch/`.

## 0.2.2 (2026-10-16)

### Changed

- **Renders leave the computer usable.** `render`'s workers are now pages in
  one browser instead of a browser each, and their encoders run on one thread,
  so a render holds about half the memory (8 workers: 1.7 GB, was 3.3 GB). By
  default it starts as many workers as the memory free fits, up to 8, and says
  so when that is fewer; `--workers N` still chooses. `render` and `check` run
  at a lower CPU priority, so the desktop and your own browser come first. On a
  machine with memory to spare, renders are as fast as before, and the frames
  are identical.

### Added

- **`pnpm launch watch`** draws a live progress bar, with the time left, for
  the render running in the launch folder, such as one Claude started:
  Claude's output is not a terminal, so there it logs a line every tenth.
  Run it in a terminal pane; it prints each render's result as it ends and
  waits for the next, until Ctrl-C.

## 0.2.1 (2026-10-16)

### Fixed

- **Installs from npm work again.** 0.1.1 and 0.2.0 were published with
  their exports pointing at TypeScript source the packages do not include, so
  in a repo installed from npm `launch.config.ts` could not load
  `@intentface-launch/core/config` and no command ran. Repos linked to a
  checkout were not affected. Update core and its add-ons to 0.2.1.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-16)

### Added

- **Type, footage and a cursor as shader input** (`@intentface-launch/shaders`).
  `useShapes` bakes a word in any loaded font (or an SVG path, or a canvas
  drawing) into the shape the library's shape effects read, so Chrome, Glass,
  LiquidMetal, Obsidian, Frost, Crystal and the rest draw your own type.
  `usePictures` and `textPicture` draw pictures and words once for
  `ImageTexture`; `shift` moves them in pixels; `clipTexture` plays a video
  file's frames, frame exact. A layer's `text` declares what it says, so
  `check` reads it, and `pointer` feeds a frame-driven pointer to
  CursorRipples, CursorTrail, Liquify and InkFlow: type turns fluid under the
  kit cursor, the same in every render.
- **Paper's shaders.** `PaperShader` runs Paper's fragment shaders
  frame-locked, and `LiquidMetal` fills any shape with flowing liquid metal,
  from polished (`matte` 0) to satin (1). `still` draws either once, as a
  picture every copy shares, so a page curl can carry a shot with liquid
  metal in it.
- **Kit.** `Flame`: a jet of fire from a point, in tongues across a fan (a
  logo's rays catching fire), coloured across it or hot to cool along it.
  `MiddleCard`: a rounded card in the middle of the frame over a shader or
  clip, frosted or as a sharp window with the surround blurred, the hero
  template. `Cursor`'s `look="frosted"`: an arrow of frosted glass, and
  `look="glass"`: clear liquid glass that refracts what is under it (DOM,
  video, shader canvases), the colours fringing where it bends.
- **Text that scales smoothly, and smoke.** `TextImage` (kit) draws a line
  of text once as a picture laid exactly where the text would be, so a
  camera can keep pushing in on an end card without the text stepping a
  pixel at a time; `picture` and `usePictures` move into the kit (the
  shaders add-on re-exports them). `Smoke` rolls smoke in from an edge until
  it fills the frame: an ending into black.
- **A book that turns, and a page curl.** `PageFlip` (kit) lays out an open
  book and turns its leaves from the frame, each leaf bending as real paper
  does, with paper you can see through (`film`, `vellum`, `parchment`,
  `paper`, `smoke`, `graphite`, `black`, or a see-through amount): what lies
  beneath shows through, blurred by the gap, light comes through the sheet,
  and it casts soft shadows tinted by what it lets through. Pages can carry
  art in depth layers that shift in parallax as they turn and redraw like
  e-ink when a leaf lands. `transitions.pageCurl` peels the outgoing shot off
  from an edge or corner, in any of those papers.
- **Examples.** `example-shaders`: the middle card both ways, fluid type,
  liquid metal.

### Improved

- **Shaders render what they are asked.** Images and baked shapes are waited
  for on every fresh renderer and URL change (a second renderer no longer
  shows an image missing), changes in how a node is composed (opacity crossing
  1, a transform starting) rebuild the layer instead of drawing before the
  library has recompiled, and simulations may run over images.
- **Shaders pre-bundled in pnpm repos.** The add-on's own dependencies (the
  Shaders library, Paper's shaders) are pre-bundled through the add-on, so in
  a pnpm product repo Vite no longer fails to resolve them at startup and
  then finds them mid-render, where the page could reload under the renderer.
- **Page turns and curls close up.** The guides say what keeps a
  see-through sheet whole at a high zoom (no texture, a light blur, only the
  pages in frame), and why repeated e-ink redraws read as flicker.
- **Paper textures by any URL.** A `PageFlip` or page curl paper's `texture`
  is quoted in CSS, so a data URL (an SVG drawn in code) or a path with
  brackets or spaces shows instead of silently going missing.
- **`check`.** `creep` only flags DOM text (a canvas that declares its text
  scales smoothly), and declared text with `bleed: true` (type larger than the
  frame on purpose) skips the safe margin.
- **Skills.** product-launch-video asks for the film's look and grade with the
  music (light or dark, palette, finish, contrast, warmth, texture, a
  reference), and music-brief checks the track's mood against it, and times
  a sung or spoken track's words with a local Whisper so cuts can land on
  them.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-15)

### Improved

- **`check` and `board` catch text crowding the UI.** A new `clearance` check
  measures the gap between each line of overlay text and the nearest panel,
  card, window or Snapshot beside it, as the viewer sees it after the camera.
  Closer than half the line's size, or touching, for half a second is an
  error, reported per shot in the board's text pass and in `check`. Text
  sitting on a surface (a label on its card, a plate behind a headline) is not
  beside it, and text on a panel is measured only against that panel's own
  contents. Set the minimum with `checks.clearance` in `launch.config.ts` (in
  ems, default 0.5), and mark a surface or line meant to touch with
  `data-launch-clearance="ok"`.
- **Skills.** product-launch-video asks for room around every line beside UI
  (at least half its size), measured where the UI lands after the camera.
  motion-review adds "text crowding the UI" to its defects and asks for a 1:1
  look at every line beside UI, since a grid tile hides a 5 px gap.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-13)

The first release: `@intentface-launch/core` (engine, kit, CLI, studio and
skills), `@intentface-launch/three` (3D) and `@intentface-launch/shaders`
(WebGPU shader effects), and the `intentface-launch` alias for `npx`.
