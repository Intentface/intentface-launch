then `pnpm launch update` to refresh the skills and guides in `launch/`.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-15)

### Improved

- **`check` and `board` catch text crowding the UI.** A new `clearance` check
  measures the gap between each line of overlay text and the nearest panel,
  card, window or Snapshot beside it, as the viewer sees it after the camera.
  Closer than half the line's size, or touching, for half a second is an
  error, reported per shot in the board's text pass and in `check`. Text
  sitting on a surface (a label on its card, a plate behind a headline) is not
  beside it, and text on a panel is measured only against that panel's own
  contents. Set the minimum with `checks.clearance` in `launch.config.ts` (in
  ems, default 0.5), and mark a surface or line meant to touch with
  `data-launch-clearance="ok"`.
- **Skills.** product-launch-video asks for room around every line beside UI
  (at least half its size), measured where the UI lands after the camera.
  motion-review adds "text crowding the UI" to its defects and asks for a 1:1
  look at every line beside UI, since a grid tile hides a 5 px gap.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-13)

The first release: `@intentface-launch/core` (engine, kit, CLI, studio and
skills), `@intentface-launch/three` (3D) and `@intentface-launch/shaders`
(WebGPU shader effects), and the `intentface-launch` alias for `npx`.
