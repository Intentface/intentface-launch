`import { … } from "@intentface-launch/core/kit"`.

## The product's UI

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Capture` | Plays a captured frame sequence, laid out at the capture's CSS size. For the product's own motion. |
| `Snapshot` | Rebuilds a saved page from its HTML and CSS, sharp at any zoom. `animate` moves rows, pills and text per frame; `api.isolate` and `api.hide` split it into layers; `api.textRects` gives the boxes of words or characters in the page's own layout, `api.type` shows a partly typed value with a caret in the product's field, and `onLoad` measures before the first frame is let go. |
| `mark`, `markRect` | A recorded mark, or the box around one or more marks, in content pixels. |
| `measureSnapshot` | Measures an element in a saved snapshot when the video loads, so it follows a recapture. |
| `useProductLook` | The product's fonts, colours and radii from a snapshot, for text set outside it. |
| `BrowserWindow` | Minimal browser chrome around product UI. |
| `Clip` | Plays a video file, or another video's render, frame exact. |

## Camera and cursor

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Camera` | Pans and pushes between keyframed views. Keys can name marks; `travel` rounds corners instead of stopping; `cut: true` jumps to a view on its frame, a cut inside the shot. |
| `cameraAt`, `useCameraView`, `frameRect`, `fit` | The camera's view at a frame, and views that fit a rect or the whole content. |
| `Cursor` | A synthetic pointer in content coordinates, constant size at any zoom. Clicks, drags, hovers. `look="frosted"` draws it as frosted glass that blurs what is under it, for glossy UI; `look="glass"` as clear liquid glass that refracts what is under it (bent in at the rim, magnified inside, the colours fringing), for material and shader shots. |
| `cursorKeys` | Cursor keys that retrace a capture's pointer. |
| `cursorAt`, `cursorClicks`, `dragWindows` | Where the cursor is, and the frames it clicks and holds. |
| `cursorSounds` | A sound on every click, ready for a shot's `sfx`. |
| `dragValue` | A value the cursor drags directly, springing from where it was let go. |
| `accelerate`, `brake` | Items landing at reading speed then faster and faster, and the shot after that slows into its framing. |

## Text

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Slam` | A word or phrase that lands on a frame with scale, blur and a fade, scaling about its baseline. Type on the beat. `axes` runs variable-font axes as it lands (letter by letter with `axesEvery`); `cutOff` marks a word the next cut interrupts, left out of the reading check. |
| `Reveal` | Staggered reveal by line, word or character: rise, mask, blur-in or scale (about the baseline). |
| `useBaseline` | Where a line's baseline sits in its box, for scaling your own type about it: about its centre, a line climbs the pixel grid a pixel at a time and judders. |
| `Typewriter` | Characters one by one, with an optional caret. |
| `typingCadence`, `typingFrames` | Uneven, human typing times, and how long typing takes. |
| `splitText`, `revealTimes` | Text split as `Reveal` splits it, and when each unit lands. |
| `Sweep` | A title built in slices that sweep in with a glint of light. |
| `Count` | A number counting up in tabular figures. |
| `Captions` | Burned-in captions, clear of the safe area and platform UI. |
| `useFontsReady`, `useMeasureText`, `measureTextNow` | Wait for a font and measure text with it. |
| `TextImage` | A line of text drawn once as a picture (at `scale`, 4 by default) and laid exactly where the text would be. A camera can keep pushing in on it and it scales smoothly; DOM text under a slow push steps a pixel at a time. Declared for `check`; `declare={false}` inside a page flip or a curl, which copy it into every strip. |
| `picture`, `usePictures` | Pictures drawn once on a canvas and kept as images every copy shares (the shaders add-on's textures use them too). |

## Paper

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `PageFlip` | An open book seen from above whose leaves turn frame by frame on `turn` (2.35: two leaves over, the third 35% of the way). Each leaf curls as it goes (the outer edge lifts and leads, the spine end lands first and the edge floats down last), is shaded across its bend and casts a soft shadow that tightens where it nears the page. A turn reads at about 2 beats on `ease.inOut`. |
| `papers` | The papers a leaf can be. Light: `film` (clear plastic: what is beneath stays sharp; a sheen and a bright edge), `vellum` (frosted: beneath shows blurred, more the higher the sheet lifts, and a lifted sheet glows), `parchment`, `paper` (opaque but for a ghost of the other side's print). Dark: `smoke` (smoked film), `graphite` (dark frosted vellum), `black` (black paper). |
| `paperFor` | A paper from how much light it lets through, 0 (opaque) to 1 (clear), between the presets (`"dark"` for the dark ones). Or override any preset: `{ preset: "vellum", seeThrough: 0.6, blur: 2 }`. |
| `PageContent` | A page is any React node, or art in layers, `{ layers: [{ depth: -2, node }, { depth: 0, node }, { depth: 5, node }] }`: each floats `depth` px off the page and shifts in parallax as the page tilts, and the page redraws like e-ink once when a leaf lands on or off it (`refresh`, in frames; 0 for none: several read as flicker). |

A see-through leaf carries a copy of what is under it in every strip of its turn. Close up (3× zoom) that ran out and strips went missing from renders for a frame at a time: keep the paper free of `texture`, its `blur` light (1 to 2 px), and give a close-up's book only the pages in frame. A small book (under about 0.7×) wants `thickness={0}`: the page block's hairlines alias into dots.

## Shaders

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Shader` | A fragment shader as a layer, painted from the frame. |
| `MeshGradient` | An animated mesh gradient that does not band. |
| `NoiseField` | Flowing noise through a colour ramp: smoke, aurora. |
| `LightGlow`, `LightLeak` | A soft light, and a light leak drifting across. |
| `FilmGrain`, `Scanlines`, `Halftone`, `Dither` | Texture over a shot. |
| `Tube`, `phosphors` | A picture you draw with Canvas 2D (`draw(ctx, frame, screen)`, at the tube's line count), shown on a modelled cathode-ray tube: phosphor that fades (`phosphor`: `standard`, `slow`, `trails`, or `long` for a scope's green tail), a beam that widens on highlights, an aperture grille, slot or shadow `mask`, curved glass, halation. Events on frames: `power` on (a line opening through snow) and off (collapsing to a fading spot), `tune` (a channel change: snow and a roll), `degauss`; `snow`, `tape` wear (jitter, a tracking band, smeared colour). The phosphor is computed by drawing the frames before, so `draw` must be pure. Text it returns is declared to `check`. For test cards, idents, teletext and 80s tape intros; `Crt` (three) puts live DOM on a lighter tube. |
| `Smoke` | Smoke rolling in from an edge (`from`) until it fills the frame with `color` on `progress` 0 to 1: billowing, curling, its thin edge lit (`light`). An ending into black, a transition into a dark shot. Drive `progress` steadily: an ease in keeps it off frame for its first second. |
| `Flame` | A jet of fire leaving a point: turbulent, flickering, white hot at the nozzle. `tongues` splits it into flames across a fan (a logo's rays catching fire), `along` colours it hot to cool as real fire burns, `grow` lights it. |
| `OilLens`, `oilDomes`, `oilShots` | A logo seen through a moving lens of oil, after Apple Music's 2026 sting: per pixel, a ray refracts through a dome of oil by Snell's law, once per wavelength, onto the mark on a dark floor. The dome magnifies it, its steep rim splits white edges into spectral fringes, a dark studio's softboxes draw the rims, waves leave its edge, and the mark shows only where the dome has reached. `oilDomes.tile` grows an orb into an app tile (an icon), `oilDomes.orb` widens an orb over a wordmark; `oilShots` are the optics each used. `ascii` draws the picture as glyphs at the dome's front and while the mark builds in (`amount`, `front`, `glitch`, your own `glyphs` and `font`); `tear` tears the picture apart for a hard glitch exit (bands pushed sideways, a split into red, green and blue or your `tint`, dropped bands); `optics.palette` splits the fringes into your colours instead of the spectrum, with white staying white. It blurs its own motion across `shutter`. Ported from Travis Fischer's study (MIT). |
| `MiddleCard` | A rounded card in the middle of the frame over a shader or clip: frosted glass blurring it (`blur="card"`), or a sharp window with the surround blurred and washed (`blur="around"`). Opens on `at`; content goes inside at the card's size. The hero template. |
