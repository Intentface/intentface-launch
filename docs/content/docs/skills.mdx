---
title: Skills
description: The skills Claude loads in a product repo, and when each one applies.
source: packages/core/skills
---

`init` installs them into the product repo's `.claude/skills/`, and
`pnpm launch update` refreshes them. They are revised as we learn.

| Skill | When Claude uses it |
| --- | --- |
| `product-launch-video` | The method, from finding what sets the product apart to the final mp4. Start here. |
| `creative-direction` | The claim, concept, script, hook, energy curve and reveal plan. When a cut feels flat or like a feature list. |
| `kinetic-typography` | Headlines, labels and end cards: hold times, entrances, staggers, syncing type to music. |
| `ui-motion` | A piece of product UI changing state: buttons, tabs, toggles, modals, toasts, counters, streaming text. |
| `micro-animations` | Choosing an easing, overshoot or spring for any small motion. |
| `logo-animation` | End cards, stings, intros and outros, with their sonic logo. |
| `motion-review` | Rendering stills and contact sheets and looking at them, after every shot. |
| `music-brief` | Writing the brief for a generated track, then cutting to its beats. |
| `sound-design` | Sound effects: spotting, a palette, generating with ElevenLabs, syncing and mixing. |
